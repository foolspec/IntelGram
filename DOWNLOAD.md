Each release attaches platform-specific SHA-256 files, validation notes, and launch logs. The [release validation workflow](https://github.com/foolspec/IntelGram/actions/workflows/intelgram-release-validation.yml) downloads the published assets and verifies their digests.

The application is named `IntelGram`. Its distinct bundle and application IDs let it coexist with a normal AyuGram installation. The macOS package is ad-hoc signed, so macOS may require Control-click, then **Open**, on first launch.

After signing in, open **IntelGram Preferences -> Local Profile & Collectibles** for local identity, the clearly labeled local public-channel showcase, and collectible rendering. Use **Appearance & Backgrounds** for visual theme previews, cloud themes, custom-theme removal, Telegram's wallpaper gallery, chat backgrounds, and app icons. Use **Glass & Motion** for adaptive Liquid Glass, native blur, a local whole-app backdrop, optical-material controls, and Quick Snap, Smooth Flow, Diabolical, Springy, or Liquid Bounce timing. Search/timeline tools, contact automation, and exports have their own focused pages so the controls are easier to find.

To enable the native local owner-style view for a broadcast channel, open its three-dot menu and choose **IntelGram -> Local ownership**. Text broadcasts appear in the real channel timeline but remain local IntelGram records. Unsupported post types are blocked before upload.

The main search field can also find already-loaded peers by UID or a phone number already visible to your account. None of these additions mutate Telegram profile data, import contacts, join a channel, obtain or change server-side channel ownership and permissions, change collectible ownership, or bypass Restrict Saving Content.
